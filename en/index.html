University of Lisbon · ISCSP

Andreia Soares e Castro

Assistant Professor · School of Social and Political Sciences (ISCSP)

International Relations · European Union · Diplomacy · Sports Diplomacy

Andreia Soares e Castro is an Assistant Professor at the School of Social and Political Sciences (ISCSP), University of Lisbon, where she has taught since 1997 and has coordinated the Master's programme in International Relations since 2018. She holds a PhD in Social Sciences, specialising in International Relations, and has been a researcher at the Centre for Public Administration and Policies (CAPP) since 2009. Her research addresses the European Union, foreign policy and diplomacy — with particular attention to sports diplomacy — and the relationship between religion and international relations, the subject of her doctoral thesis.

III.

Sports Diplomacy in Focus

One of her most distinctive research lines examines how states use major sporting events to project soft power:

  • The 2018 FIFA World Cup: The Gains and Constraints of Russia's Soft Power of Attraction Through Football and SportsRising Powers Quarterly, 3(3), 2018, pp. 17–37Analyses Russia's use of the 2018 World Cup to strengthen its soft power and international image.
  • South Africa's Engagement in Sports Diplomacy: The Successful Hosting of the 2010 FIFA World CupThe Hague Journal of Diplomacy, 8(3–4), 2013, pp. 197–210Examines South Africa's success in using sport as a diplomatic instrument.
  • 2014 FIFA World Cup and 2016 Olympic Games: Brazil's Strategy «To Win Hearts and Minds» Through FootballPublic Diplomacy Magazine, 2013Studies Brazil's strategy of international projection through mega sporting events.
